Visual walkthrough

What the pipeline looks like in practice

From generating a role to a recruiter applying flexible, custom judgment during scoring — every step below is a real screen from the working app, not a mockup.

1

Define the role

Overview

Landing page

The project overview — problem statement, the pipeline visual, tech stack, and what's real vs. simulated.

Generate

Job description generator

A rough role idea turned into a structured job post, plus a distilled key-skills list used later for retrieval.

Persist

Saved job descriptions

Generated roles persist to MySQL, along with optional mandatory skills, preferred skills, and minimum experience defaults.

2

Build the candidate pool

Add

Add a candidate from free text

Type a short background description — an LLM extracts structured skills and writes a professional resume summary automatically.

Browse

Candidate pool

Every candidate's skills, experience, and summary, browsable at a glance — including anyone added manually.

3

Score, two ways

RAG

Automatic, retrieval-filtered scoring

Semantic retrieval narrows the pool before scoring — every result ships with a written explanation, not just a number.

Custom

Recruiter-defined criteria

Set mandatory skills, preferred skills, minimum experience, and free-text guidance for how flexible the AI should be.

Result

Judgment-aware scoring in action

This path intentionally skips retrieval and scores the full pool — so a candidate with adjacent, not exact, skills still gets fairly considered when the recruiter says it's okay.

4

Prep, review, and act

Prep

Job-aware interview briefing

Strengths, gaps to probe, and interview questions tailored to both the candidate and the specific role.

History

Previous scoring, on demand

Pull up saved scores for any role instantly, without re-running the AI — a quick way to revisit a past decision.

Outreach

Shortlist & human-approved outreach

High scorers surface for review; a recruiter manually approves before an AI-drafted email goes out.

Sift — portfolio project, not affiliated with any commercial recruiting platform.
